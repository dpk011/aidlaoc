AID is proud to be the charity partner for Nachle Deewane 2021, , hosted by the [Dhadkan](http://www.dhadkan.org/) at UC Santa Barbara (UCSB).

Nachle Deewane 2021 is an annual Bollywood &amp; Bhangra Dance Competition.

The event proceeds will go to AID for projects and campaigns centered around COVID-19 relief support.

Save the date and time, **April 10, 2021,   5 PM PST** for this free online event.
